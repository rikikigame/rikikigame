Cookies and browser storage

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In brief

  • Rikiki sets no cookies for tracking, advertising or statistics.
  • The game stores small pieces of necessary data in your browser (who you are, your current game) and your settings (sound, language, card deck, large text…). They are never sent to advertising networks.
  • Two items load only after your agreement: Google reCAPTCHA and KLIPY GIFs.

1. Why we do not ask for consent for everything

In Belgium (article 129 of the law of 13 June 2005 on electronic communications) and in the European Union, your agreement is not needed to store information that is strictly necessary for a service you asked for, such as keeping you signed in or remembering a setting you chose (language, sound…). It is needed for everything else. That is why the two outside services listed below only load after a click from you.

You can erase everything at any time in your browser settings ("clear site data"). You will then have to sign in again.

3. What loads only after your agreement

Outside services that depend on your choice

You can change these two choices at any time: ⚙️ Settings in the game. Withdrawing an agreement prevents later loading; it does not delete what Google or KLIPY already received.

4. What is loaded from outside anyway

The Google Firebase programs are loaded from Google's server (gstatic.com): this is essential for the online game. The pages of the site come from GitHub Pages. These two companies see your IP address like any website. More details in the privacy policy. Fonts are hosted by Rikiki itself.
