Oh Hell score sheet and scoring

In Oh Hell (also called Rikiki, Nomination Whist or Blob), you write down each player's bid every round, the number of tricks they actually won, and their points. Here is the scoring, some examples and a printable sheet.

▶ Or let the app keep score: play Rikiki

Scoring used in online Rikiki

  • Bid made (you won exactly your bid): 4 points + 4 points per trick won.
  • Bid missed: −2 points per trick of difference between your bid and your result, whether you won too many or too few.

Examples

  • You bid 2 and win 2: 4 + 2 × 4 = 12 points.
  • You bid 0 and win 0: 4 points.
  • You bid 2 and win 3: one trick off, so −2 points.
  • You bid 3 and win 0: three tricks off, so −6 points.

This is the app's default scoring and the host can change it before the game starts. Other scoring systems exist: for example 10 points plus 1 per trick when you make your bid, and nothing when you miss. Agree on one before you start.

Printable score sheet

Write each player's name at the top. For every round, write the Bid, the Tricks won and the round Pts. The number of rounds depends on the number of players: you deal from 1 card up as long as the 52-card deck is enough (for example 13 rounds with 4 players).

Let the app keep score for you

Playing around a table with a real deck? Pick "Real cards" on the game's home screen: the app keeps the score sheet, adds up the points and shows the ranking. You can also play online with friends or against bots, for free.

Frequently asked questions

How do you score Oh Hell?

Making your bid earns a fixed bonus plus points per trick; missing it costs points for every trick of difference. Default scoring in the app: 4 + 4 per trick if made, −2 per trick off if missed.

How many rounds in a game?

You start with 1 card and go up while the 52-card deck is enough: 13 rounds with 4 players, for example. A quick game is 5 rounds.
