In brief

Rikiki is a free game between friends. Be fair to others, do not cheat, do not try to break the game. If you do not follow these rules, your access may be restricted or removed.

1. Who are we, and what do these terms cover?

Rikiki is a free online card game published by K.vroom (contact: admin@rikikigame.com). These terms apply when you use the game's site and app. By creating a profile (or by playing), you accept them. They should be read together with the privacy policy.

2. Who can play?

Rikiki is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, ask a parent for permission. The game is free: no payment is requested on the site. The "Buy me a beer" link opens an outside site (Buy Me a Coffee); it is optional and gives no advantage in the game.

3. Your profile

  • Choose a nickname that does not look like your real name and that is not offensive, misleading or an imitation of someone else's nickname.
  • Your secret code is personal: do not share it. You are responsible for what is done with your profile.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your profile, or ask for it to be deleted (see the privacy policy).

4. Rules of good conduct

It is forbidden to:

  • cheat (for example try to see other players' cards, or play with several people on one account to distort a ranking);
  • exploit a flaw, use an automatic program, or try to disrupt the game, the database or other players;
  • write messages or send GIFs that are hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, violent or illegal;
  • publish personal information about yourself or other people (name, address, phone number…);
  • use the game for advertising or solicitation.

GIFs come from KLIPY, an outside service: Rikiki does not control their content. If one shocks you, write to us.

5. Moderation

The publisher may, without notice in case of abuse: hide a game from the league, put a ranking back in order, restrict or delete a profile. The publisher may also stop or change the game at any time.

The privacy policy explains which data is used and what your rights are. Rikiki sets no tracking cookies (see the cookies page).
